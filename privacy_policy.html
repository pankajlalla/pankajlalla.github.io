Privacy Policy for Myocoach

Last updated: September 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, access, use, storage, and disclosure of your information when you use the Myocoach mobile application (the "Application" or "Service") and informs you of your privacy rights and legal protections.

We use your Personal and Health Data to provide and improve the Service. By using Myocoach, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

  • Application (or "Service"): Refers to Myocoach, the fitness and athletic tracking mobile software program.
  • Company ("We", "Us", or "Our"): Refers to Myocoach (Rajasthan, India).
  • Device: Any mobile device that accesses the Service, including Android smartphones, smartwatches, and tablets.
  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Health Data: Data related to physical fitness, athletic training, physiological biometrics, body composition, sleep, and physical activity collected through user input, device sensors, connected Bluetooth wearables, or the Android Health Connect platform.
  • Health Connect: Google's on-device platform and API on Android that enables applications to securely read, write, and synchronize health and fitness data with user consent.

2. Comprehensive Health and Fitness Data Disclosure

Myocoach is a workout logging, strength training, and athletic conditioning application. To deliver features such as real-time biometric telemetry, training volume tracking, recovery baselines, and personalized workout analytics, Myocoach accesses, collects, and processes specific Health Data.

A. Specific Categories of Health Data Collected & Accessed

  • Exercise and Workout Sessions (READ_EXERCISE, WRITE_EXERCISE): Exercise types, sets, reps, weight lifted, workout duration, rest intervals, and workout timestamps.
  • Heart Rate and Biometrics (READ_HEART_RATE, READ_RESTING_HEART_RATE): Real-time continuous heart rate during active workouts and daily resting heart rate.
  • Activity and Movement Metrics (READ_STEPS, READ_DISTANCE, WRITE_DISTANCE, ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): Daily step count, distance traveled during cardio/workout sessions, and physical activity recognition state.
  • Energy Expenditure (READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED, WRITE_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED, READ_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED, WRITE_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED): Active calories burned during exercise sessions and total estimated daily energy expenditure.
  • Sleep and Recovery (READ_SLEEP): Sleep session duration and sleep intervals to estimate physical recovery readiness.
  • Body Composition and Biometrics (READ_HEIGHT, READ_WEIGHT, READ_BODY_FAT): Body weight, height, and body fat percentage (e.g. from smart scales, connected devices, or manual logs).

B. How We Access and Collect Health Data

  1. Direct User Input: Information you manually record in Myocoach, such as logged exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, body weight, or height.
  2. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Wearables: With your explicit permission (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT), Myocoach connects to paired heart rate monitors and smartwatches to stream real-time heart rate telemetry during active workouts.
  3. Android Device Sensors: Utilizing the device's physical activity sensor (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION) to detect workout motion and step progress.
  4. Google Health Connect APIs: With your explicit runtime permission, Myocoach reads historical and baseline health data (steps, sleep, resting heart rate, body composition) and writes completed workout sessions, distance, and calorie expenditure back to Health Connect.

C. How We Use Your Health Data

  • Workout Tracking & Progression: Logging exercise performance, calculating total training volume, and generating progressive overload recommendations.
  • Heart Rate Zone Monitoring: Displaying real-time heart rate and cardiovascular effort zones during workouts.
  • Metabolic & Caloric Calculations: Calculating energy expenditure for completed workouts and syncing burned calories back to your health profile.
  • Recovery & Readiness Insights: Analyzing sleep duration, resting heart rate baselines, and step averages to provide contextual training fatigue insights.
  • Body Composition Tracking: Visualizing changes in weight, body fat percentage, and lean muscle mass over time.
  • Two-Way Ecosystem Synchronization: Writing your Myocoach workout sessions to Health Connect so other authorized health apps can access your workout data, and reading data from devices (e.g. smart scales) to populate your Myocoach profile.

Medical Disclaimer: Myocoach is designed solely for fitness, athletic training, and personal wellness monitoring. Myocoach is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Always consult a qualified medical professional before starting any new exercise routine.

3. Google Health Connect Limited Use Disclosure

Myocoach's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google Health Connect APIs adheres to the Google Play Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  1. Permitted Use Only: We only use Health Connect data to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the Myocoach user interface.
  2. Prohibition on Advertising: Health Connect data is never used for advertising, interest-based ad targeting, retargeting, user profiling, or personalization of ads.
  3. Prohibition on Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or transfer Health Connect data to third parties, data brokers, advertising platforms, or information resellers under any circumstances.
  4. Strict Isolation from Advertising SDKs: Although Myocoach displays general banner/interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob, Health Connect data, biometric records, and health data are strictly isolated and are NEVER shared with, accessed by, or transmitted to Google AdMob or any other advertising SDK.
  5. Human Access Restrictions: We do not allow human beings to read user Health Connect data unless:
    • We have obtained your affirmative explicit consent for specific messages (e.g. customer technical support or troubleshooting);
    • It is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse or technical bugs);
    • It is required to comply with applicable laws; or
    • The data is aggregated and anonymized for internal system diagnostics.

4. Other Data Collected and Device Permissions

A. Personal Account Data

When you create an account or back up your data, we may collect your email address, name/display name, and authentication tokens (e.g., via Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth).

B. Camera Access

With your explicit runtime permission (CAMERA), Myocoach accesses your device camera solely to scan QR codes (e.g. gym equipment linking, profile sharing, or syncing workout routines). All QR processing is performed strictly on-device in real-time. Myocoach does not photograph, record, store, or transmit camera images or video feeds to any remote server.

C. Bluetooth & Nearby Devices

Permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) are requested exclusively to scan for and connect to Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) fitness accessories (heart rate straps, smart bands) during active workouts. We do not use Bluetooth for location tracking or marketing beacons.

D. Technical & Usage Diagnostics

We automatically collect standard diagnostic data including device model, operating system version, app version, crash traces, and basic anonymous performance diagnostics to ensure application stability.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We never sell your personal or health data. We only share information under the following limited circumstances:

  • Cloud Storage & Infrastructure: We use secure cloud services (e.g., Google Firebase) to store authenticated user accounts and encrypted backups. These service providers act strictly as data processors under binding confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if legally required by valid subpoenas, court orders, or governmental regulations.
  • Advertising Isolation: While general usage metrics and device identifiers may be processed by advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) for non-premium users, NO Health Data, Health Connect data, or biometric data is ever shared with or accessible to advertising partners.

6. Data Security and Storage

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security controls to protect your health and personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication between the application and backend servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 / HTTPS).
  • On-Device Security: Health Connect data resides in Android's secure, hardware-backed on-device datastore.
  • Access Controls: Cloud-backed data is accessible only through your authenticated credentials.

  • Revoking Health Connect Permissions: You can review or revoke Myocoach's access to Health Connect permissions at any time directly through your device settings:
    Android Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Health Connect → Apps with access → Myocoach.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account, workout history, and all associated personal and health data. You can initiate deletion by:
    1. Using the in-app account deletion option in Settings, or
